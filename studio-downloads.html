Studio Downloads

Download a room, import it, and start your setup sheet with the mic locker, outboard rack, preamps, and floor plan already in place.

To use a pack, open Setup Sheet Helper and go to Settings, Import/Export, Import Studios. Imported rooms land as Custom Studios, so they sit alongside anything you have already.

Want your room in here? Send an export from Settings, Import/Export, Export studios to the contact form.
